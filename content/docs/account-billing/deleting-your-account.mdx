Changed your mind? You can delete your Nomu account and everything in it—profile, trips, places, photos, and settings—whenever you like.

## Delete from the app

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Go to **Settings → Privacy and data**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

## The 30-day grace period

Deletion isn't instant—your account is **scheduled for deletion in 30 days** rather than wiped on the spot. During that window:

- Your account is deactivated and your data is no longer visible in the app.
- **Changed your mind again?** Sign back in and choose to keep your account—everything is restored, no harm done.
- After 30 days, your account and all its data are permanently deleted and can't be recovered.

Think of it as a safety net, not a countdown to stress over.

## Can't get into the app?

Email **[support@nomu.land](mailto:support@nomu.land?subject=Delete%20my%20account)** from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. The same 30-day grace period applies.
