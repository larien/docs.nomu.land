Getting started is simple: open Nomu, enter your email, and Nomu sends you a **six-digit code**. Type it into the app and you are in—no extra password to memorize or lose behind the couch. Nothing else is needed to join: the beta is open.

The code lasts **10 minutes** and works once. If the email plays hide-and-seek, do not panic: [I didn't receive the sign-in code](/troubleshooting/sign-in-code-not-received) walks through the usual fixes (spam folders, typos, grumpy corporate filters).
