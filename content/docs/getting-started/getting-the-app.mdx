Nomu is on **Android**. Grab it from Google Play, sign in, and your trips are with you wherever you go.

## Android

<Cards>
  <Card title="Get it on Google Play" href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=land.nomu.app" />
</Cards>

Or open **Google Play** and search **Nomu travel**. Install it, launch Nomu from your home screen, and sign in.
