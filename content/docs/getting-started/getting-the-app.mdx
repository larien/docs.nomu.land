Nomu lives on your phone. The **Android app** is out in open beta; iOS is on the way.

## Android

Install from Google Play:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Get it on Google Play" href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=land.nomu.app" />
</Cards>

Or open **Google Play** and search **Nomu travel**. Once installed, launch Nomu from your home screen and sign in with your email.

You can also open **[nomu.land/get](https://nomu.land/get)** from any phone: it sends you to the right place for the device you are holding.

## iOS

<Callout title="Coming soon">
  An iPhone app is on the way. Leave your email on [nomu.land](https://nomu.land)
  and you will hear on the day it lands.
</Callout>

## Tips

- Your account is the same one everywhere; sign in once and your trips follow you to any device you install Nomu on.
- Links to **nomu.land/open** open the app directly when it is installed.
- [Sharing a map link or place into Nomu](/getting-started/share-into-nomu) from another app needs the app installed.
