Want Nomu on your phone? The **Android app** is live—grab it from Google Play and sign in with the same account you use in the browser. Everything syncs; there's no separate “app login.”

## Android

Install from Google Play:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Get it on Google Play" href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=land.nomu.app" />
</Cards>

Or open **Google Play** and search **Nomu travel**. Once installed, launch Nomu from your home screen and sign in.

## iOS

<Callout title="Coming soon">
  An iPhone app is on the way. Until it lands, open **[app.nomu.land](https://app.nomu.land)** in Safari—same account, same data.
</Callout>

## Desktop

No install needed—open **[app.nomu.land](https://app.nomu.land)** in your browser and you're set. (The marketing site on nomu.land is separate.)

## Tips

- You stay on the same account everywhere; sign in once and your trips follow you.
- On mobile, [sharing a map link or place into Nomu](/getting-started/share-into-nomu) from another app is often smoother with the app installed.
