title: Collaborative trips
description: Planning a trip with other people—what is possible today.
updated: 2026-08-17
---

Planning a trip together is not available yet: a trip belongs to the account that created it, and there is no way to invite someone else to edit it.

What you can do today is **share** a finished trip. [Sharing a trip](/trips/sharing-a-trip) covers the card you can send to anyone, no account needed on their side.
