---
title: Using Nomu without signal
description: What Nomu can do without a connection, and what still needs one.
updated: 2026-08-17
---

Nomu is built to stay **useful offline after you have used it online**: what you have already opened is kept on your device, so a plane, a tunnel or a foreign SIM does not empty the app.

## What usually works

- Opening the app and reading trips and places you have already loaded.
- Anything already on the device from your last online session.

## What still needs a connection

- Map tiles you have not looked at yet.
- Searching for new places, which asks the server.
- A **fresh install with no online session yet**—there is nothing stored to read.

## If the app looks empty offline

Get back online for a moment and open your trips list once, so the app has something current to keep. Then try again without signal.
